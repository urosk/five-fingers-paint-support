Five fingers. Five colors. One canvas.

Five Fingers Paint is a simple drawing app made for children. Pick five colors, place up to five fingers on the canvas, and paint with every finger independently.

Getting started

Pick a color and paint.

The controls stay deliberately small so the canvas belongs to the painting.

  1. 1

    Choose the first color

    Tap one of the five paint puddles. The last one tapped becomes the color for single-finger drawing.

  2. 2

    Build your palette

    Pull the paintboard upward, select a puddle, then tap a bottle to replace that color. Flick the board down when you are done.

  3. 3

    Paint with one to five fingers

    The first finger uses the active color. Each extra finger gets another palette color and keeps it until it lifts.

  4. 4

    Clear or save

    Tap the brush, then drag it upward to clear. Tap the easel to save the canvas to Photos.

Touch input

Every finger stays itself.

Five Fingers Paint tracks direct screen touches independently, without creating a trail of short-lived objects as you draw.

Stable colors

Each active finger occupies a fixed slot. Moving fingers quickly or crossing their paths does not swap their colors.

Natural brush size

Stroke width follows the finger’s measured contact radius and narrows slightly during faster movement. It does not depend on 3D Touch.

Room to draw

Starting a stroke closes the color shelf. Drawing near the bottom temporarily hides the controls, which return after the stroke ends.

For parents

Keep the iPad in the app with Guided Access.

Guided Access is Apple’s built-in child-friendly mode. It prevents accidental app switching while leaving all five drawing touches available.

Before handing it over: To prevent saving, open Settings → Five Fingers Paint and turn off Show Save to Photos Button.

  1. Open Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, turn it on, and set a passcode.
  2. Open Five Fingers Paint and triple-click the top or Home button.
  3. Leave Touch enabled, then tap Start.

FAQ

Touch, gestures, and controls.

Does the app really support five fingers at once?

Yes. The canvas accepts up to five simultaneous direct touches. Each finger is tracked independently from touch-down until it lifts.

How are colors assigned to fingers?

The first finger uses the last paint puddle you tapped. Additional fingers receive the other unused palette colors in order. A finger keeps its assigned color for the whole stroke, even when paths cross.

Is painting pressure-sensitive?

It responds to contact size, not pressure. iPhone and iPad report the approximate radius of each touch, and the app uses that to vary brush width. It also narrows the brush slightly during fast movement. This preserves the original effect without relying on the discontinued 3D Touch hardware.

Do I need to grant permission for five-finger drawing?

No. Multi-touch is a normal iOS capability and requires no privacy permission. Five Fingers Paint enables it directly on the canvas.

Can iPad system gestures interfere?

The app asks iPadOS to defer edge gestures, and its own paintboard and clear controls are kept away from the canvas. However, iPadOS can still reserve four- or five-finger gestures for switching apps. Guided Access is the reliable way to prevent that during a painting session.

How do I keep a child inside the app?

Use Apple’s Guided Access. Enable it once in Settings → Accessibility, open the app, triple-click the top or Home button, leave Touch enabled, and start the session. This locks the device to Five Fingers Paint while preserving multi-touch drawing.

Can Five Fingers Paint enable Guided Access automatically?

No. A normal App Store app cannot start Guided Access for you. A parent must enable and start it using the device controls. Automatic Single App Mode is limited to supervised, organization-managed devices.

Why do the controls disappear near the bottom?

They move out of the way so a stroke can continue beneath them. The outline briefly marks where they were, and the controls return shortly after all fingers leave the screen.

How do I clear the canvas?

Tap the brush beside the paintboard. When the red dashed arrow appears, drag the brush upward toward the paper until the canvas clears.

How do I save a painting—and why might the button be missing?

Tap the easel to save a copy to Photos. The app requests add-only Photos access, so it cannot browse the library. Parents can hide or show the easel in the iOS Settings app under Five Fingers Paint.
